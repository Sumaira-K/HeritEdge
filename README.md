# HeritEdge

## From Generation to Generation

HeritEdge is a community-driven digital heritage platform designed to capture, process, structure, and preserve India's living cultural heritage.

Existing digital heritage platforms provide valuable resources for cultural discovery, documentation, archiving, research, and education. However, the process of capturing grassroots oral traditions and transforming them into structured, searchable, and transferable cultural knowledge remains fragmented.

HeritEdge addresses this gap by providing an AI-assisted workflow that helps communities document and preserve cultural knowledge at the grassroots level.

---

## Problem Statement

India's cultural heritage exists across institutional archives, cultural portals, social platforms, and community records.

A significant amount of cultural knowledge, however, remains undocumented or exists only in unstructured forms such as:

* Oral histories
* Local traditions and customs
* Regional-language recordings
* Community practices
* Traditional recipes
* Crafts and artisan knowledge
* Festivals and cultural practices
* Place-based stories
* Audio, video, images, and text

The core problem is not the absence of digital heritage platforms.

The problem is the **fragmentation of the heritage preservation journey**.

---

## Solution

HeritEdge introduces a community-driven workflow for converting raw cultural knowledge into structured digital heritage records.

### Workflow

```text
Capture
   ↓
Transcribe
   ↓
Translate
   ↓
Extract Cultural Knowledge
   ↓
Create Structured Heritage Record
   ↓
Connect to Place & Community
   ↓
Preserve & Transmit
```

The platform combines multimedia capture, AI-assisted processing, regional-language support, structured heritage records, community validation, and preservation within a single workflow.

---

## Key Features

### Multimedia Heritage Capture

HeritEdge supports the documentation of cultural knowledge through multiple formats:

* Audio
* Video
* Images
* Text

This allows communities to document living traditions using the format most appropriate to the knowledge being preserved.

### AI-Assisted Processing

AI is used to assist in processing cultural content through:

* Speech transcription
* Translation
* Cultural knowledge extraction
* Information structuring

The objective is to reduce the effort required to transform raw recordings and content into usable cultural knowledge.

### Structured Heritage Records

Rather than preserving cultural knowledge only as isolated media files, HeritEdge focuses on converting captured information into structured heritage records.

This makes cultural knowledge easier to organize, preserve, search, and potentially transfer across digital heritage systems.

### Regional-Language Processing

HeritEdge incorporates regional-language processing into the documentation workflow, supporting the preservation of cultural knowledge in the languages in which it is naturally expressed.

### Community Validation

Community participation remains an important part of the preservation process.

HeritEdge supports community validation of captured knowledge, ensuring that AI-assisted processing complements community knowledge rather than replacing it.

### Place and Community Connection

Cultural heritage is closely connected to the communities and places where traditions originate and continue to exist.

HeritEdge aims to connect heritage records with relevant communities and geographical context.

## What Makes HeritEdge Different?

HeritEdge does not attempt to replace existing cultural heritage institutions or platforms.

Instead, it addresses a complementary part of the ecosystem: **grassroots capture and structuring of new cultural knowledge**.

Existing platforms are strong in areas such as:

* Digital cultural collections
* Cultural discovery
* Institutional archiving
* Research
* Documentation
* Education
* Cultural presentation

HeritEdge focuses on:

* Grassroots cultural capture
* Everyday living heritage
* AI-assisted processing
* Regional-language processing
* Automatic cultural knowledge extraction
* Structured heritage record generation
* Community validation
* Capture-to-preservation workflows

The competitive analysis identifies the combination of these capabilities within a single workflow as HeritEdge's primary differentiation.

## Relationship with UNESCO

HeritEdge complements the UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage framework.

UNESCO primarily focuses on recognizing, documenting, safeguarding, and promoting significant intangible cultural heritage.

HeritEdge operates further upstream by helping communities capture and structure living cultural traditions before they are formally recognized or documented.

This creates a community-driven layer that can help generate, organize, and preserve cultural knowledge that might otherwise remain undocumented.
